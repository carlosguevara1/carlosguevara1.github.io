I am a Peruvian economist currently pursuing the Master of Arts in Economics (MAE) at the <a href="https://economics.uchicago.edu/academics/ma-program" target="_blank">University of Chicago</a>.

Before beginning the MAE, I worked as a Research Analyst on the United States Desk at the <a href="https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/USA" target="_blank">International Monetary Fund</a>, where I contributed to research and policy work on the U.S. economy. Earlier, I worked at the <a href="https://www.iadb.org/en/about-us/departments/res" target="_blank">Inter-American Development Bank</a> and the <a href="https://www.bcrp.gob.pe/en" target="_blank">Central Bank of Peru</a>.

I also contribute to open-source econometrics. I am co-author of <a href="https://github.com/d2cml-ai/csdid" target="_blank">csdid</a>, the Python implementation of the staggered difference-in-differences estimator of <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304407620303948" target="_blank">Callaway and Sant'Anna (2021)</a>. My contributions include treatment-effect aggregation and visualization tools. The package has now surpassed <strong>400,000 downloads</strong>.

I was born in Cusco and grew up in Peru, where I studied Economics at the <a href="https://www.pucp.edu.pe/carrera/economia/" target="_blank">Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú</a>. I am particularly proud to be the first in my family to graduate from university. That background has shaped both my ambitions and the way I approach my work.

Beyond economics, I enjoy photography, salsa dancing, international food, and movies. I am also endlessly inspired by the work ethic of <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6odPP0zAdFQ&ab_channel=Footballer%27sStories" target="_blank">Cristiano Ronaldo</a>, the economics of <a href="https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/samuelson-lecture.pdf" target="_blank">Paul Samuelson</a>, and the creativity of <a href="https://www.paradigmpress.org/as/article/view/447" target="_blank">Quentin Tarantino</a>.
